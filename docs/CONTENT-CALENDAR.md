## 2. The 4-Phase Viral Distribution Playbook

Over 90% of festival greeting traffic originates from mobile devices, predominantly via WhatsApp groups and individual chats. Execute this distribution sequence for every festival:

```
[Phase 1: Week -4 to -3] -> SEO Publication & Search Console Indexing
[Phase 2: Week -2 to -1] -> Community Seeding & Pinterest Visual Pins
[Phase 3: Day -3 to Day 0] -> Peak Viral Loop (WhatsApp Status & "From Amit" Sharing)
[Phase 4: Day +1]         -> Retargeting & Next Festival Carousel Handoff
```

### Phase 1: SEO Indexing (Weeks 4 to 3 Before Event)
1. Publish `blogger-post.html` on your custom domain / Blogger page.
2. Submit URL directly in Google Search Console via **URL Inspection** -> **Request Indexing**.
3. Create 3 to 5 Pinterest pins with high-contrast vertical cards linking to `/p/<festival>.html`.

### Phase 2: Seeding & Pre-Buzz (Weeks 2 to 1 Before Event)
1. Seed the link with family and friends:
   `"Hey, look at this cool greeting card generator for Dussehra: <URL>?to=Rahul&from=Amit"`
2. Post a sample personalized greeting to your WhatsApp Channel and Telegram groups.
3. Invite users to customize their cards early for family members.

### Phase 3: The Peak Viral Surge (Day -2 to Festival Day)
1. **WhatsApp Status:** Post the 1080x1920 generated status image with a direct link sticker.
2. **The "Make One For Someone Else" Viral Loop:**
   - User A sends to User B: `wa.me` link carries `?to=UserB&from=UserA`.
   - User B sees their name in glittering letters.
   - User B taps "Make One for Someone Else".
   - The engine automatically puts `UserB` in the `from` field and prompts them to enter their parents' or friends' names.
   - Each recipient becomes a sender, generating exponential viral multiplier loops ($K$-factor $> 1.2$).

### Phase 4: Carousel Handoff (Day +1)
- The page's "Upcoming Festivals" carousel immediately cross-links visitors to the next festival on the calendar (e.g., Dussehra -> Diwali -> Chhath Puja), driving retention and repeat traffic.
